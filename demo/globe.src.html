Where on the planet

A demo, not the app. Build a trip: say where you are flying from, then add stops. The globe turns to them, the countries light up, and the journey is flown leg by leg — the flight in, then every hop between the cities, by whatever that hop actually uses. Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to stand back.

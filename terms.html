What بوصلة is

بوصلة (Bosla) is a travel-planning tool. It builds suggested itineraries — places, restaurants, hotels, budgets, and travel times — from curated data and AI assistance. Everything it produces is a suggestion, not a booking and not advice. Opening hours change, restaurants close, prices move, and visa rules are yours to verify. Always confirm details with the venue, your airline, and official sources before you travel or pay anyone.
